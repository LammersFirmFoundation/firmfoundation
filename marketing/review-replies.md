# Review replies

Google lets the owner answer every review in public, and people deciding
whether to call read the answers as closely as the reviews. Google's own
advice: reply, keep it short and specific to the review rather than one
generic thank-you for everyone, and never put personal details in a reply ([Google Business Profile Help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122)).
Written 2026-11-01. The Google profile is managed from Will's Google account
(business.google.com, then Reviews), so Will or Josiah can paste these.

## How to reply

- Within a few days of the review.
- In Josiah's voice, signed "Josiah". Two or three sentences.
- Mention the actual job, so it reads like a person and not a template. Don't
  paste the same reply twice.
- Never mention a price, an address, a last name or anything private.
- Never offer anything in return for changing or removing a review.
- A bad review gets one calm reply and a phone number. Never argue in public.
- Only report a review to Google if it breaks Google's rules (spam, someone
  who was never a customer, off-topic or abusive). A negative review from a
  real customer stays.

## Templates for new reviews

Fill the brackets with the real job. Change the wording each time.

**Five stars, with details**
> Thank you, [first name]. [One line about their job, for example: "The new beds along the front walk came out well, and I'm glad you like them."] I appreciate you taking the time to write this. Josiah

**Five stars, no words**
> Thank you, [first name]. I appreciate the five stars. Josiah

**Four stars**
> Thank you, [first name]. I'm glad [what went well]. If there's anything we could have done better, I'd like to hear it: (843) 998-5593. Josiah

**Three stars, or a mixed review**
> Thank you for the honest review, [first name]. You're right about [the specific problem], and [what we've changed, or what we'll do]. I'd like to make it right, so please call me at (843) 998-5593. Josiah

**One or two stars, a real customer**
> [First name], I'm sorry the [job] didn't turn out the way you expected. I want to understand what happened and fix what I can. Please call or text me at (843) 998-5593. Josiah

**Someone who was never a customer**
> We don't have a record of working with you, but I'd like to understand what happened. Please call me at (843) 998-5593. Josiah

Then report it to Google from the review's menu, choosing the reason that
fits (for example, a conflict of interest or not a real experience).
