# Shot list: photos and video from every job

Josiah's own photos beat any stock photo on the website, and they are what
Instagram now rewards. A before photo can't be taken after the job starts, so
this list is about the first five minutes on site. Written 2026-11-01.

## Every job, every time

1. **Before you start, stand somewhere you can find again** (the mailbox, a
   corner of the house, a fence post) and take three photos:
   - wide: the whole area;
   - medium: the part that will change most;
   - close: the problem itself (the low spot, the stump, the bare dirt).
2. **Phone sideways for these.** Sideways photos go on the website, and the
   website has a slot waiting for each kind of job below.
3. **Then one 10-second clip with the phone upright** for Instagram Reels.
   Slowly, from left to right. No talking needed.
4. **Halfway through, one photo of the part that gets buried:** the base under
   a patio, the pipe in a French drain, the hole for a pool. Nobody can see it
   afterwards, so it is the best proof of doing it right.
5. **When it's finished and cleaned up, go back to the exact same spots** and
   take the same three photos and the same upright clip.
6. **Text Will the before and after the same day.** Both photos, in one text.

**Small things that help:** wipe the lens on your shirt first; keep the sun
behind you; don't zoom, walk closer; hold the phone at chest height both times.

**Keep out of every shot:** house numbers, street signs, license plates, and
people's faces, especially children. Ask the customer before anything of
their house goes online. A text that says yes is enough.

## By job

| Job | Before | Halfway (the buried part) | After |
|---|---|---|---|
| New beds and planting | The beds as they are, from the walk or the street | New plants set out in place before they go in | Same spot; then one close-up of a new plant in fresh mulch |
| Paver patio or walkway | The bare area, from the house looking out | The compacted stone base with the edge restraint, before sand and pavers | From the house looking out and from the yard looking back |
| Retaining wall | The slope | The first course level on its base, and the stone behind it | Straight on, and from the end |
| Grading | Water standing after a rain, or the ground sloping toward the house | The machine shaping the grade | After the next rain, from the same spot, with no water standing |
| French drain or swale | The standing water | The trench with fabric, stone and pipe, before backfill | Sod back down, from the same spot |
| Pool or pond dig | The staked layout | The machine mid-dig, with the hole half done | The finished dig, ready for the pool builder |
| Land clearing | From one fixed spot at the edge of the lot | The machine working | From the exact same spot |
| Stump | The stump | The root ball coming out, or the grinder at work | The spot backfilled and level |
