Built from Josiah's own photos and the website's own drawings and guides; every fact in a caption comes from the website or the source it names. The rules for posting are in `../../posting-rules.md`.

**How to use it.** On Sunday evening, schedule the week's four posts from the Instagram app (on the last screen before sharing, open the advanced settings and choose to schedule) or from Meta Business Suite. Turn on sharing to Facebook once the Page is linked. Copy the caption exactly, add the alt text to each image, tag the location "Mount Pleasant, South Carolina", and post at the time listed. The images and videos are in `images/` and `reels/`, named by post number.

**Why these times.** Sprout Social's March 2026 data puts the best Instagram windows at Tuesday and Wednesday afternoons, Monday 2 to 4 PM and Thursday 12 to 2 PM, with weekends lowest ([Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/best-times-to-post-on-instagram/)). Buffer's 2026 data favours weekday mornings and evenings ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/best-time-to-post)). After 30 days, check Instagram's own Insights for when his followers are online and move the times to match.

**Why five hashtags.** Instagram limited posts to five hashtags in December 2025, and the town name in the caption now does more than tags for Instagram search.

**Three posts need Josiah's own video** (06, 12, 17). Each has a short filming brief and a fallback if the clip isn't ready.

```
Before you cut down a tree in Mount Pleasant, measure it.

On a residential lot the Town protects trees 16 inches or more across, measured 4 1/2 feet off the ground, and pines from 24 inches. A short list of species, sweet gum among them, never needs a permit.

The request goes through the Town's online permit system and costs $50. Take a protected tree down without one and the Town fines both the homeowner and whoever cut it. That's why we ask about the permit before anything goes on the calendar.

The full answer, with the Town's own rules, is on our website under Guides.

#mountpleasantsc #charlestonsc #lowcountry #treeremoval #stumpremoval
```

## 10. Tue 17 Nov, 1:00 PM · Carousel · How it's built

**Media:** `images/10-1.jpg`, `images/10-2.jpg`

**Caption** (copy all of it):

```
If water sits against your house after it rains, check which way the ground slopes.

Building code asks for the ground to fall at least 6 inches in the first 10 feet away from a house (International Residential Code, R401.3). When it slopes toward the house instead, rainwater runs back to the foundation.

The fix is regrading: cut the high side, fill and pack the low side, and leave the finished grade falling away from the house.

Not sure which way yours runs? Text us a photo after the next rain: (843) 998-5593.

#mountpleasantsc #charlestonsc #excavation #grading #drainage
```

**Alt text:** (1) Section drawing at a house: the existing grade falling toward the house, the finished grade falling away from it, with at least 6 inches of fall in the first 10 feet. / (2) Text a photo of your yard to (843) 998-5593 for a free on-site quote.

Fall is the best season to plant shrubs here. Here's how deep they should go.
